Model state

Deductive: each county's counted votes are held fixed exactly as reported. Only the still-uncounted remainder is projected, blended between that county's own results and the baseline at a credibility weight that grows with how much has reported, and shifts further when many counties show the same pattern, not just one. The runoff/advance probabilities above shrink their uncertainty as the state fills in and collapse to a hard outcome once counting finishes.
